Research
The science behind every feature
Shot Geometry & xG
Tactical pitch shows real-time angle & distance. xG calculated from goal-mouth angle (Sumpter formula) plus GK and defender pressure.
  • Sumpter, D. (2017). The geometry of shooting. Soccermatics. link
  • de Boode, V. (Ajax). Goalkeeper reaction time research cited in Sumpter (2017).
Keeper Positioning
GK positions on the bisector line between shooter and goal-center, then closes down within 3m (FA "3-metre rule").
  • FIFA Training Centre / Dittmer, T. — Goalkeeper's positioning in & out of possession. link
  • Top Goalkeeping (2025). Goalkeeper Positioning: Where to Stand In Match Situations.
Decision Window / Temporal Occlusion
Countdown bar mimics real time pressure. Shots taken under low time = lower decision-quality score. Backed by occlusion-paradigm meta-analysis showing transfer to real performance.
  • Broadbent et al. (2024). Accelerating Visual Anticipation in Sport Through Temporal Occlusion Training: A Meta-Analysis. Sports Medicine. link
  • Hohmann et al. (2018). Effects of Video-Based Visual Training on Decision-Making and Reactive Agility in Adolescent Football Players. PMC.
Perception–Action Cycle
Shooter must position, scan GK, decide zone, execute — modeled on the three-stage cognitive cycle of elite play. Random GK movement & defender recovery force in-the-moment recalibration.
  • Vestberg et al. (2024). Cognition in elite soccer players: a general model. Frontiers in Psychology. link
  • Dicks et al. (2010). Perception–action coupling in goalkeepers (lab vs. field).
Cognitive Training Apps That Inspired This Build
Live HUD & scenario randomization influenced by Rezzil; cognitive metrics (decision quality, scan rate concept) from Be Your Best.
  • Rezzil — VR cognitive sports trainer used by Premier League clubs. link
  • Be Your Best — VR scanning & decision-making for soccer (28% scan-rate gain in 9 weeks). link
  • Soccer IntelliGym — computer-based decision training. link
  • A-Champs ROX / Goal Station — multi-sensory perception-action pods.
Heatmap & Pattern Recognition
Zone heatmap helps you avoid predictability — pattern recognition is a known expert advantage.
  • Williams et al. (2012). Pattern recognition expertise in soccer.
  • Mann et al. (2007). Perceptual-cognitive expertise as a defining feature of expert performance.
